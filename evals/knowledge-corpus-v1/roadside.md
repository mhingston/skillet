# Roadside Assistance

Roadside assistance helps members after a vehicle breakdown when the car cannot continue safely.

## Eligibility

A member with active roadside cover is eligible to request assistance. If a motorist is stranded and the vehicle cannot be repaired at the roadside, towing can be arranged under the applicable cover.

## Towing destination

The recovery destination depends on the member's cover and the operational circumstances. Do not infer entitlement from the breakdown location alone.
